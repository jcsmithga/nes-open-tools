## Seed Generation
* "Course Mode" replaces Total Par.  Modes:
    * Standard (Par 72): Four par 3s, ten par 4s, four par 5s.  The current default.
    * Natural Par (68-76): The par is rolled for each seed and is usually near 72.
    * Executive Course: Par 3s plus two to six par 4s, with no par 5s. Par is 56-60.
    * Par 3 Course: Eighteen par 3s. Total par of 54.
    * Vanilla Course: One course from either version of the game, holes in 1-18 order.
* Hole mirroring and hazard shuffling are new settings: "Each hole mirrored" and "Each hole hazards shuffled". The choices are Off (the default), Unlikely (25%), Coin flip (50%), Likely (75%) and Always.
* The expert limit has a new "Two per Nine" option.

## Seed Page
* Course overview puts small "m" (for mirrored) and "hs" (for hazards shuffled) tags beside a hole's id.
*  Several changes to settings table to reflect new generation settings.
