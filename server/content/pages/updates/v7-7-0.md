## Daily Seed Play
* A match now ends when one player leads by more holes than are left to play.
* Match results on the daily seed page now give the result in golf notation.
* Each result links to a new "Match Card", a traditional match play scorecard for both players.
* The Results section headings now name the formats: Net Stableford and Net Match Play.
* Past matches are rescored under the new rules.

## Site Formatting
* A new "Yesterday" box sits under today's daily, with the podium finishers from
that day's Net Stableford competition.
* On small screens the calendar is now a list of the month's days, newest first.
* Greens in regulation have been added to scorecards.
* Scorecard layouts have been generally improved.  FIRs and GIRs are dropped
when rendering on small screens.
